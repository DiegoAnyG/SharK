Calculation details and reproducibility

Blue/red (or the configured phase colors) show the sign of the orbital amplitude, not electric charge. A global sign reversal has no physical meaning. Bond lines indicate inferred connectivity.

The available isovalues were generated from the original scalar grids without amplitude normalization. To add values, regenerate this report with a settings JSON. A single grid does not establish resolution convergence.
