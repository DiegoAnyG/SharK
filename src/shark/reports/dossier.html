What is recorded

Method, charge, spin, geometry source, execution state and available frequency checks. Orbital fields retain their source export; inspect provenance before comparing calculations.

What remains model dependent

Orbital energies and gaps depend on the functional, basis, solvent and molecular state. A HOMO–LUMO gap is not an activation barrier, optical gap or covalent-binding score. A single CUBE grid does not establish spatial convergence.

What would test covalency

A specific reactive atom pair, chemically complete QM region and environment, reactant/product structures, a reaction path and transition-state checks. Multiple conformations and competing mechanisms are needed to assess robustness.

Classical trajectory contacts can nominate geometries for QM/MM. Neither contact persistence nor isolated-ligand frontier orbitals establish that a covalent bond forms.

ORCA multiscale methods describe QM/MM boundaries and reaction-path tools. GROMACS QM/MM documentation distinguishes fixed-connectivity classical models from quantum treatment.
